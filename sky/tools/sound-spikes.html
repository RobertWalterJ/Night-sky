Sound test

Four short tests that tell us how your phone plays sound, so Sky Sound can line up with the map and keep playing with the screen off. About 10 minutes. Use a comfortable volume. Nothing leaves your phone unless you press Copy at the end and paste it to Claude.

1. Check this phone

Reads what your phone says about its audio.







  
2. Screen-off test

  
Does sound keep playing when the screen turns off? Pick a way to play, tap Start, then press the power button to turn the screen off. Wait 20 seconds. Turn the screen on again.

3. Line up sound and flash

  
You will hear a click once a second and see the box flash. Move the slider until the flash and the click feel exactly together. Then tap Save.

4. Measure the delay with the microphone (optional)

  
Use the phone speaker, not headphones, in a quiet room. You will hear 10 short chirps. The microphone listens and works out how long each way of playing takes. It stores nothing and sends nothing. If it asks for the microphone, tap Allow.

  
  






  
5. Speed test

  
Checks how much work different sound effects ask of your phone. Takes about 15 seconds. Stay on this page.
